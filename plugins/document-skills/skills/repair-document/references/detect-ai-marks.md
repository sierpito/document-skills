# AI marks (all formats)

Applies to .pptx, .docx, .pdf, .html and .md. Look for, and list with slide, page or part name:

- **Visible symbols:** sparkle/star glyphs (U+2728, U+2726, U+2733), a "Generated by", "Created with",
  "Made with AI", "AI-generated", "Powered by" line, a tool logo or corner badge, a stray
  placeholder like "[Insert ...]", "Lorem ipsum", "Sample text".
- **Watermarks:** semi-transparent text or picture shapes repeated on every slide or on the master or
  layouts (`alpha`/`<a:alphaModFix>`, rotated text, a full-slide picture), and text named
  "Watermark" in `cNvPr`.
- **Metadata:** tool or vendor names in `docProps/app.xml` (`Application`, `AppVersion`),
  `core.xml` (`description`, `keywords`, `lastModifiedBy`), `docProps/custom.xml`, shape names and
  alt text ("Generated image", "AI image").
- **Image provenance:** for each image (`ppt/media/*`, `word/media/*`, PDF images): EXIF/XMP Software, `DigitalSourceType`
  (`trainedAlgorithmicMedia`), C2PA/JUMBF manifests (`c2pa`, `jumb` boxes), PNG text chunks
  (`parameters`, `prompt`), filenames like `ChatGPT Image`, `DALL-E`, `Midjourney`, `Gemini_Generated`.
  Read with Pillow or `strings`; never upload images anywhere.
- **Notes and alt text** carrying prompts or chat residue.

Report marks; do not delete a disclosure, licence or watermark that states AI use. Delete marks that
are build leftovers (placeholder text, stray sparkle glyphs, empty "Powered by" lines) and say so. What may
be removed from image metadata, and what never is, is stated once in `invisible-and-metadata.md`, section 3.
